# Player photos (not committed)

Drop photos here in which every person who may play is labelled with their name (a white box
with the name next to them is fine). Several photos are fine; they are combined.

When the app starts a game, it reads these once, cuts out each labelled person, stitches them
into one roster sheet, and uses that sheet to recognise who is playing.

These files are git-ignored on purpose: they show real faces and names, and this repo is public.
Share them with the team privately, not through git.
