# House rules: 8-ball (plain, for now)

The referee reads this file before every shot. Edit it to change the rules; no code changes needed.

## The balls

Every ball has its own number and colour. As soon as a number can be read on a ball, the number
decides which ball it is. The colour then confirms it, and so does solid vs stripe.

| Number | Colour | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| cue (no number) | white | cue ball |
| 1 | yellow | solid |
| 2 | blue | solid |
| 3 | red | solid |
| 4 | purple | solid |
| 5 | orange | solid |
| 6 | green | solid |
| 7 | maroon (brown-red) | solid |
| 8 | black | solid colour, but it is the 8-ball, in neither group |
| 9 | yellow | stripe (white ball with a yellow band) |
| 10 | blue | stripe |
| 11 | red | stripe |
| 12 | purple | stripe |
| 13 | orange | stripe |
| 14 | green | stripe |
| 15 | maroon | stripe |

- Each colour except black and white appears twice: once solid (1-7), once striped (9-15).
- A stripe seen from above can show mostly its white cap, so don't mistake it for the cue ball. The
  cue ball has no colour at all.
- There is exactly one cue ball and one 8-ball, at most 7 solids and at most 7 stripes.

## The game

- 15 object balls: solids 1-7, stripes 9-15, and the black 8. Plus the white cue ball.
- The break is the first shot of a game. The table stays open after the break, whatever is potted.
- Open table: the first player to pot a ball legally after the break takes that group (solids or
  stripes). The other player gets the other group.
- A player keeps shooting while they legally pot a ball of their own group (or any ball on an open
  table). Otherwise the turn passes to the other player.
- Fouls. The turn passes and the other player gets ball in hand:
  - the cue ball is potted (a scratch) or leaves the table;
  - the cue ball hits no ball;
  - the first ball the cue ball hits is not one of the shooter's group (on an open table, hitting
    the 8 first is a foul).
- Balls potted on a foul stay down.
- Once a player has potted all 7 balls of their group, they shoot the 8. Potting the 8 legally wins.
- A player loses if they pot the 8 before clearing their group, or scratch while potting the 8.
- No called pockets.
